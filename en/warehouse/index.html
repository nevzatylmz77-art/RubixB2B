What is RubixB2 WMS?

RubixB2 WMS is a smart warehouse management solution integrated with ERP that lets you carry out daily warehouse operations with mobile devices and barcode scanning.

By managing core warehouse tasks such as goods receipt, order picking, stock counting and barcode checks digitally, it reduces manual data entry and helps prevent operational errors.

Warehouse staff work directly on mobile devices, bringing speed, accuracy and traceability to your warehouse processes.

Features

Key features

01📥

Mobile Goods Receipt

Receive incoming goods on mobile devices. Verify products with barcode checks and manage your receiving process faster and more reliably.

02🧺

Barcode-Supported Order Picking

Pick the products for customer orders by scanning their barcodes. Make sure the right products are picked and reduce errors in picking and shipping preparation.

03🔢

Mobile Stock Counting

Carry out warehouse stock counts on mobile devices. Barcode-supported counting makes it easier to check physical stock, and count results are managed integrated with Odoo ERP.

04🔍

Barcode Check & Product Verification

Look up product barcodes with mobile devices to check product information. Make sure you work with the right product in receiving, picking and stock counting.

05👷

Mobile, User-Friendly Operations

Let warehouse staff carry out their tasks right on the floor using mobile devices. Reduce paper-based processes and increase operational efficiency.

Integration

Warehouse management integrated with ERP

Every warehouse transaction, aligned with your central system.

RubixB2 WMS works with Odoo ERP's inventory and warehouse processes so that warehouse operations run integrated with the central system.

Because receiving, picking, stock counting and product verification are done on mobile, manual data entry is reduced while stock accuracy and operational traceability increase.
