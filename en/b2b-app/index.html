What is the Branded B2B Mobile App?

The Branded B2B Mobile App is an iOS and Android ordering solution integrated with B2B eCommerce and developed specifically for your corporate identity.

It strengthens your existing Odoo B2B web shop with a mobile app, so your customers can easily browse your products, place orders and view their past orders and invoices.

Customised with your company name, logo and corporate identity, the app is published on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store under your own brand.

Key features

🏷️

A Mobile App Built for Your Brand

Offer your customers a professional, branded digital shopping experience with a mobile app designed around your corporate identity. Your app is published in the app stores under your company name and logo.

🔗

Odoo B2B eCommerce Integration

Bring the products, customers, pricing and order processes of your existing Odoo B2B web shop to your mobile sales channel. Manage web and mobile sales together on the same ERP.

🖼️

Slider & Campaign Management

Promote your campaigns, new products and special announcements on the app's home screen with visual sliders. Easily manage the content your customers see with the Slider Management System.

🔔

Push Notifications

Send mobile notifications straight to your customers about new products, campaigns, promotions and important announcements. Communicate quickly and effectively to create more sales opportunities.

🧾

Order & Invoice History

Let your customers easily access their previous orders and past invoices in the mobile app. Improve the customer experience with the full order history in one place.

🔁

Reorder

Let your customers repeat previous orders with ease. Speed up ordering and make repeat purchases easier, especially for B2B customers with regular supply needs.

♥

My Favourite Products

Let your customers add frequently purchased or interesting products to their favourites. Quick access to favourites shortens the time it takes to place an order.

📱

iOS & Android Support

With an app that runs on iOS and Android, your customers can place orders wherever and whenever they want.

Integration

Mobile B2B sales integrated with ERP

Your web and mobile sales channels on one ERP.

The Branded B2B Mobile App works with your Odoo B2B eCommerce infrastructure and brings your existing digital sales system to mobile platforms.

Product catalogues, customer-specific pricing, order history and invoice information are presented in line with your Odoo setup.

Your business can offer customers a new digital ordering channel by building on its existing Odoo investment, without having to run a separate mobile sales platform.
