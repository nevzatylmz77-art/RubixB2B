FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between van sales and pre-sales?

In van sales, the sales rep delivers the product from the van immediately, issues the document and collects payment. In pre-sales, the rep only takes the order; delivery is done later by the warehouse and shipping team. RubixB2 supports both models, separately or together.

Does it work with our existing Odoo system?

Yes. RubixB2 is designed to run on Odoo. Your existing product, customer, price and stock data is used directly. We review version and implementation details together during the demo.

Does it require an internet connection?

Yes. RubixB2 is a fully online system. Every transaction in the field is saved to Odoo instantly, so stock, price and account information is always up to date both in the field and at the office. A phone or tablet with an internet connection is all you need.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on your team size, your current Odoo setup and the customisations required. After a needs analysis we give you a clear project plan.

Which reports can I get?

Target vs. actual, visit and route compliance, product distribution, collections, returns and sales rep comparison reports come out of the box. Custom KPIs and dashboards can also be designed.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the number of users and the modules you choose. Just fill in the demo request form for a tailored quote.

Which infrastructure do you use for location and maps?

RubixB2 uses Google Maps for maps, addresses and routing. The sales rep's position is taken from the device's GPS and matched with the customer's address on Google Maps, so visits are verified with high accuracy and routes are planned on the map.
