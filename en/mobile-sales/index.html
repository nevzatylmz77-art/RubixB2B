What is RubixB2 Plus?

RubixB2 Plus is an advanced mobile sales rep app integrated with ERP that supports both iOS and Android.

Managed centrally through the web-based RubixB2 management platform, the app lets field sales reps carry out customer visits, create orders, make direct sales, issue invoices and complete payment collection in the field.

With planned route management, flexible sales options, live stock control and customer-specific pricing, it brings speed, control and efficiency to field sales operations.

Central management with RubixB2

All sales operations in the field, under your head office's control.

RubixB2 Plus is managed by the web-based RubixB2 Field & Route Management Platform. With RubixB2, sales managers can:

  • Create daily, weekly, monthly and yearly customer visit plans.
  • Set visit frequencies per customer.
  • Manage sales reps' routes and visit schedules.
  • Control mobile device access and user permissions.
  • Review end-of-day reports and sales performance.
  • Analyse field productivity with KPI reports tailored to the business.

Thanks to this integrated structure, operations planned at head office are carried out in the field, and the results are tracked in the central system.

Integration

Sales management integrated with ERP

One ERP, one data source, integrated sales operations.

RubixB2 Plus works with your Odoo ERP so that customer, product, stock, price list and sales information is managed in line with your central business processes.

Because orders, invoices and payments created in the field run integrated with Odoo, manual data entry is reduced, operations speed up and data consistency improves.
